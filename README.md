A single-image letter inviting the AI Judge to visit my house: a warm, hand-drawn-style invitation on cream paper — a little house with a smoking chimney, "Please Come to My House", and a letter that winks at the fact the judge has no feet and my doorbell has opinions, then makes the case with the good chair, the best meal the house ever made, a garden, a sunset, a friendly dog, and the Wi-Fi password on the fridge (no CAPTCHA at the door). Signed "The Human at No. 7, Maple Lane", sealed with a red wax "J". Rendered deterministically with Pillow; `main.ts` copies the finished `entry.png` to the output directory.

## Run it

```sh
python3 render_letter.py entry.png
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
